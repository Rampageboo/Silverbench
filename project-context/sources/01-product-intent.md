# Product intent

Recover inspectable and correctable Canonical Humanoid Motion from anime or
game-character video. Offline quality, uncertainty, diagnostics, and limited
human correction take priority over one-click output or real-time performance.

M0 is contract-only. It must not contain inference, model downloads,
proprietary assets, a full UI, fingers, face, clothing physics, or production
exporters.

Target deployment is offline batch inference on an RTX 5070 Ti Laptop GPU with 12 GB VRAM. The RTX 2060 in the current development workstation is a compatibility-test device, not the product performance target.
